# In-Depth Comparative Review and Pitfall Guide to Global Affiliate Networks for Indie Hackers

As a software engineer, when choosing an affiliate network, you must avoid the traditional mindset of selling physical goods. What we actually need are: **SaaS-heavy platforms, support for monthly Recurring Commissions, API access, and developer-friendly onboarding for Indie Hackers.**

## II. Core Platform Features & Actionable Playbooks

### 1. PartnerStack — The Undisputed King of B2B SaaS
* **Why It’s the Top Choice:** The world's most commercially valuable tech software lives here. It completely discards the messy commission deductions of traditional networks, offering absolute transparency on payment cycles.
* **Onboarding Tips:** Select "Promoter" during registration. For your website URL, fill in your GitHub profile or tech blog. Under promotional methods, check "Content / Developer Communities / Social Media"—you'll usually get approved instantly.

### 2. Rewardful — The Hidden Empire of Micro-SaaS
* **How It Works:** Instead of a centralized marketplace, Rewardful operates as the underlying SDK embedded directly into the landing pages of thousands of independent software products.
* **The Footer Hunting Trick:** Whenever you spot a great indie tool (e.g., AI face-swappers, Twitter schedulers, SEO analyzers), scroll straight down to the footer, press `Ctrl + F`, and search for **`Affiliate`** or **`Partner`**. Clicking it redirects straight to the Rewardful sign-up page. Drop in your email, and you'll instantly get your custom tracking short link.

### 3. Impact.com — The Hub for Enterprise & Global Brands
* **Core Advantage:** High-profile international heavyweights (like Shopify) exclusively host their affiliate programs on Impact. Its reporting granularity is the most detailed in the industry, even offering dedicated APIs for engineers to pull click and conversion data programmatically via scripts.
* **Pitfall to Avoid:** After joining Impact, you still need to apply to each brand individually. It's recommended to first apply to 1 or 2 brands with low entry barriers to generate real conversion data before tackling enterprise giants like Shopify.
